Calculation

Spesenfix calculates by the German rules for domestic business trips (Income Tax Act, section 9 (4a) and section 4 (5)). The rates are a table per year inside the app, not part of the program code. They are listed here as the app uses them. This is not tax advice. The law, the letters of the Federal Ministry of Finance and your employer's travel policy are what counts; check the rates before you rely on a result.

Meals per calendar day

  • One-day trip: more than 8 hours of absence give €14.00, up to 8 hours €0.00.
  • Multi-day trip: arrival and departure day €14.00 each, regardless of duration; each full day in between €28.00.
  • Deduction: for every meal provided (breakfast, lunch, dinner) the amount from the table is deducted. The deduction of a day is never higher than that day's allowance, the daily amount never below 0.

Example: a departure day (€14.00) with breakfast and lunch provided: breakfast €5.60 and lunch €11.20 would be €16.80 together. The deduction is capped at the allowance: €5.60 + €8.40 = €14.00, the day results in €0.00.

Overnight stays and mileage

  • With a hotel invoice the invoice is entered as a receipt. For the same night the overnight allowance is never added.
  • The overnight allowance of €20.00 per night is meant for private stays and is under Add › More items.
  • Mileage: distance times rate, rounded to cents. Example: 120 km by car = €36.00.

Receipts and VAT

  • Amounts on receipts are gross, VAT is included: VAT = gross × rate / (100 + rate), rounded to cents. Example: €231.80 at 19 % contains €37.01 VAT.
  • VAT rates: 19 % (regular), 7 % (reduced), 0 %. The preset follows the receipt type (7 % for example for long-distance train tickets, books, ferry tickets and taxi, otherwise 19 %). It is a suggestion; the receipt decides, and you can change the rate per receipt.
  • Several VAT rates on one receipt: the receipt is split into items. The amount is the sum of the items, VAT is calculated per rate and added up.
  • A receipt the company already paid counts in the total of receipts and is deducted again from the reimbursement.

Business meals (section 4 (5) no. 2 Income Tax Act)

  • Required details: date, place, occasion, participants (internal and external), amounts. They produce the page “Details zur Bewirtungskostenabrechnung” in the PDF.
  • The tip is on its own line and carries no VAT.
  • 30 % of the net amount of the meal is not deductible (gross minus VAT, without the tip). The line only appears when external guests were invited.

Example: bill €122.00 gross at 19 %: VAT €19.48, net €102.52, 30 % of it = €30.76 not deductible. A tip of €13.00 is added without tax; total €135.00.

Reimbursement

Reimbursement = overnight allowances + meal allowances − deductions + receipts + mileage − receipts paid by the company − cash advance

What the app does not calculate

  • Trips abroad and foreign rates, foreign currencies (everything is calculated in euro).
  • Whether a trip counts as a business trip for tax purposes (first place of work, three-month rule and the like) and the treatment at the employer (tax exemption, payroll tax).
  • Specifics of your employer's travel policy.

Expense Id

Each report gets a number when it is created, in the format YYMMDD-XXXXXXXX: the creation day and 8 random characters (without look-alikes such as 0/O or 1/I). It is made on the device and stored with the report; two people on the same day very probably get different numbers (about 1 in a million with 1,000 new reports a day), without a central authority there is no guarantee.
