Report ideas and bugs

How

  • Open an issue on GitHub: templates for an idea and a bug. You need a GitHub account.
  • Without GitHub: through the TestFlight app (“Send Feedback”) or by e-mail to mobile_bumbles_6z@icloud.com.
  • Please no real receipts, names, amounts or credentials in public issues. Describe the case with invented values. The log from the app (Settings › Diagnostics) contains no such data; feel free to attach it.

Outlook (ideas, no promise)

  • Trips abroad with foreign rates and currency
  • More work on several items and currencies per receipt
  • A “How it was calculated” view with the working per day
  • A Control Center and Lock Screen control for scanning
  • Automatic deletion in the background as well
  • Accessibility: a full check with VoiceOver
