FAQ

Which devices does the app run on?

On iPhone and iPad with iOS 27 or later and on the Mac with macOS 26 or later. Sync through your own iCloud.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere except your own iCloud (when you are signed in) and wherever you send a PDF yourself. There is no account, no server of the developer, no advertising and no tracking. Receipt recognition runs on the device. Details are in the privacy policy.

Can the developer see my reports?

No. The data lives on your devices and in your iCloud. The diagnostic log contains no names, amounts or receipts and leaves the device only when you export it yourself.

Why Germany and euro only?

The rules are implemented for domestic trips. Trips abroad and foreign currencies are an idea for later, see Ideas.

Does the app replace tax advice?

No. It calculates by the rules on the page Calculation. Check the result and your employer's policy.

Why do I have to mark the report as submitted myself on the Mac?

On the Mac your mail app opens. The app does not learn whether the mail was really sent. On the iPhone it notices when you send with the mail sheet.

Can I use Outlook?

Yes, under Settings › Send with. On the iPhone Outlook opens with recipient, subject and text; you attach the PDF yourself from “Files › Spesenfix”. On the Mac Outlook opens a message with the PDF attached. Sending directly through a company account is deliberately not offered.

What is the Inbox?

A collecting place for receipts that do not belong to a report yet: scanned, shared from Mail, Photos or Files, or imported on the Mac. You assign them later.

How long are reports kept?

You choose 1 or 3 years. 30 days before deleting a notice with export appears. Submitted, unpaid reports are never deleted automatically.

How do I report a bug?

Through the TestFlight app (“Send Feedback”), as an issue on GitHub or by e-mail. Helpful: what you did, what happened, and the log from Settings › Diagnostics › Export Log. No real receipts or names in public issues.

Is the app accessible?

The app uses the standard system elements (text size, light/dark, accessibility settings). A full check with VoiceOver is still to be done.
