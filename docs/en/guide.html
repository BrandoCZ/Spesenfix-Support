Guide

Spesenfix creates travel expense reports for Germany: record the trip and receipts, the app calculates meal allowances, overnight allowances and mileage by the German rules, and you send the PDF by mail. The app runs on iPhone and Mac in German and English.

First start

  1. Enter your first and last name and your company. Both are printed in the header of the PDF.
  2. Create at least one cost center, with the supervisors who approve reports for it.
  3. The recipient address for the mail is optional. Enter it here or later in Settings; if it stays empty you type the recipient when you send.

Profile and cost centers are in Settings (iPhone: gear at the top left, Mac: menu Spesenfix > Settings).

Creating a report

  1. New Report (button at the bottom, on the Mac also ⌘N): choose title, cost center and supervisor.
  2. Trip: destination, purpose, start and end. “Recent places” offers earlier destinations.
  3. Days: for each day switch on the meals that were provided (breakfast, lunch, dinner) and choose the overnight stay (none, allowance or hotel receipt). The app reduces the allowance automatically.
  4. Add receipts: receipt type, date, amount, VAT rate and whether the company paid (“Paid by company”). The suggested VAT rate follows the receipt type.
  5. The reimbursement is always shown at the bottom of the report.

Another similar trip? Swipe a report to the right (Mac: right-click) and choose Duplicate. The copy is a new draft with the same days, moved to today, without receipts.

Receipts from a photo or PDF

A receipt has an Attachments section: choose a photo, import a file, drop it on the Mac, or photograph it with Scan Document on the iPhone (or scan first and assign later, see below). Attachments are printed at the end of the PDF.

The app reads the attachment on the device and suggests amount, date, VAT rate, merchant and, when it is clear, the receipt type under Found in the attachment. Apply puts them in. Always check the values: the recognition is a suggestion. For multi-trip tickets the app suggests the price of one trip because only one trip is submitted.

Scan now, assign later

On the road you do not need a report. Tap Scan at the bottom of the iPhone and photograph the receipt. It lands in the Inbox; the app reads merchant and receipt type in the background.

Later open the Inbox and choose Add to Report…: a new report or a draft. The receipt opens with the recognised values, you check and save. The scan then disappears from the Inbox. Inside a report you can also use Add > From the Inbox; scans from the time of the trip come first. On the Mac, put files into the Inbox with Import…, by dropping them on the window or with File > Import from iPhone or iPad.

The number on the Inbox tab shows how many scans are waiting.

Scan faster

Press and hold the app icon: Scan opens the camera at once, even when the app is closed; New Report and Inbox go straight there. The same actions exist in the Shortcuts app and for Siri, and you can put them on the Action button: Settings › Action Button › Shortcut › Spesenfix › Scan. The entries appear after the first start of the app.

Share into the Inbox from other apps

In Mail, Photos, Files or Safari tap Share on a PDF or image and choose Spesenfix. The file lands in the Inbox as a scan without opening the app (each file is its own scan). Then proceed like with a scanned receipt: Add to Report. If Spesenfix is not in the row of apps, swipe the row to the right, choose More (or Edit Actions) and switch Spesenfix on. On the Mac the share extension works the same way from Mail, Preview, Finder and Safari.

Receipt types and business meals

The receipt types match those of common travel expense systems (for example hotel invoice, train ticket, taxi, parking, fuel, postage). The most common ones are at the top of the picker, the rest under More receipt types in alphabetical order.

The type Business meal has a second step: occasion, place, internal and external guests and tip. It produces the page “Details zur Bewirtungskostenabrechnung” in the PDF. The non-deductible part is calculated automatically.

Report without a trip

For single receipts (for example a business meal or a team evening) leave the destination empty. The report then contains receipts only, and the PDF is created and sent the same way. The app remembers per receipt type whether the company already paid (train, hotel) and suggests it next time. For a business meal the menu offers earlier entries for occasion and place.

PDF and mail

Open PDF in a report. You see the preview and can choose Send by Mail. The recipient comes from Settings, the subject is the title of the report, the PDF is attached.

  • iPhone with Outlook: choose Outlook under Send with in Settings. Outlook opens with recipient, subject and text. Outlook cannot take the PDF automatically: the app saves it in its folder and you attach it in Outlook with the paperclip from Files › Spesenfix. Then mark the report as submitted (the app asks in the PDF window). Without Outlook the app says so and offers Share PDF.
  • iPhone with the share sheet: choose Share sheet under Send with. The system share sheet opens with the PDF and text; tap Outlook (or another app). What Outlook prefills depends on Outlook. The log (Settings › Diagnostics › Export Log) shows which app you chose.
  • Mac with Outlook: choose Outlook under Send with. Outlook opens a new message with the PDF attached; you enter recipient and subject there.
  • iPhone: after sending with Mail the report becomes Submitted automatically. Without a mail account the app offers Share PDF.
  • Mac: your mail app opens. Mark the report as submitted yourself (Mark as Submitted) because the Mac does not learn whether the mail was really sent. The action can be undone.

Progress and next step

At the top of each report the band shows where you are: Data, Check, Sent, Paid. The big button at the bottom adapts: Add Receipt, Check and Send, later Mark as Paid.

In the list each report has a line saying what comes next, for example “Ready to send”, “Trip ended 4 days ago” or “Open for 12 days, usual: 8”. Orange means it takes longer than usual. Amounts are deliberately not shown there. Swipe a submitted report to the right to mark it as paid (today's date, changeable in the report).

Rare cases are under Add › More items: mileage with a private vehicle and the overnight allowance for a private stay. Otherwise the overnight choice is “None / hotel receipt”.

Several VAT rates on one receipt (for example a hotel invoice with 7 % for the room and 19 % for breakfast): tap Several VAT Rates… in the receipt and enter description, amount and VAT rate per item. The amount of the receipt is the sum of the items. In the report it stays one row; the VAT is shown per rate on its own line.

Status: Draft, Submitted, Paid

  • Draft: in progress.
  • Submitted: sent, money not received yet.
  • Paid: you mark the report yourself with Mark as Paid and choose the payment date. Several reports at once with Mark Several as Paid… (Mac: ⇧⌘P). The date cannot be before the submission.

The app icon shows the number of submitted, unpaid reports once you have allowed notifications. The app asks once after you submitted your first report.

Statistics

Statistics (Mac: ⌥⌘S) shows how long payment takes on average (average, median, fastest, slowest), the reimbursements per month, cost center, supervisor and receipt type and the amount still open. Export as CSV… saves the data for a spreadsheet.

Reminders

Under Settings › Reminders you choose what the app reminds you of: a scan still in the Inbox after 3 days, a trip that ended 2 days ago while the report is still a draft, and a submitted report that waits longer than usual for payment. At most one notification per day (default 9:00) with everything that is open. Days and time can be changed, everything can be switched off. The app asks for the notification permission after your first scan.

Retention and deleting

Under Settings > Retention you choose 1 or 3 years (default 3).

  • Paid reports count from the submission date, drafts from creation.
  • Submitted, unpaid reports are never deleted automatically.
  • 30 days before, the Deletion notice appears at the top of the list (and a notification, if allowed). There you can Export… (ZIP with all PDFs and a summary as CSV), Keep for One More Year or Delete Now.
  • Deleting only happens after the notice has been shown for at least 30 days, and only at start or when you open the app again.
  • With Keep statistics (default) a deleted report leaves only dates, cost center, supervisor and amounts for the statistics. Title, destination, receipts and files are gone. Delete Kept Statistics… removes that too.

If something does not work

In Settings under Diagnostics you find Export Log…. The log shows what the app did and contains no names, amounts or receipts. You can pass it on by mail or message. Clear Log removes it.

Good to know

  • The tax rules apply to domestic trips in Germany. Rates and formulas are on the page Calculation. The app is not tax advice.
  • All amounts are in euro.
  • Type size and appearance follow the system settings (text size, light/dark).
