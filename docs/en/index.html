Reisekosten

Travel expense reports for Germany on iPhone and Mac: capture the trip and receipts, scan receipts, calculate per-diems, accommodation allowances and mileage, and send the report as a PDF by mail. The app is currently in testing (TestFlight).

Support and feedback

The easiest way to report bugs, ask questions or suggest changes is the TestFlight app (share a screenshot, “Send Beta Feedback”). You can also write to mobile_bumbles_6z@icloud.com. Please do not post real receipts, names or credentials in public GitHub issues.

Privacy

The app has no account, no advertising, no tracking and no server of its own. Details: privacy policy.
