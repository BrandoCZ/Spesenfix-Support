Spesenfix

Travel expense reports for Germany on iPhone and Mac: record the trip and receipts, scan receipts, let the app calculate meal and overnight allowances and mileage by the German rules, and send the report as a PDF by mail.

Status: beta (TestFlight), version 0.7.0. The app is not in the App Store yet.

What the app does

  • Trip and days: meal allowances with deductions for meals provided, overnight allowance, mileage.
  • Receipts: all common receipt types, photo, PDF or scan as attachment, several VAT rates on one receipt, business meals with the required evidence.
  • Scan on the go: receipts wait in the Inbox and are assigned to a report later; share into the Inbox from Mail, Photos, Files or Safari.
  • Reading receipts: amount, date, VAT rate, merchant and receipt type are recognised on the device and offered as a suggestion.
  • PDF and mail: the report as a PDF in a fixed layout, sent with Mail, Outlook or the share sheet.
  • History: status Draft, Submitted, Paid; statistics with payment times and CSV export; reminders.
  • iCloud: sync between iPhone, iPad and Mac through your own iCloud. No account, no server of the developer.

Requirements

  • iPhone and iPad with iOS 27 or later, Mac with macOS 26 or later.
  • German and English; domestic trips in Germany only, all amounts in euro.
